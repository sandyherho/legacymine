Symbols, units, and reference values
====================================

units
-----
  All quantities are SI except where a figure axis states MPa, kPa s,
  or ms. Depth z is positive downward from the sea surface; r is the
  horizontal distance from the vertical axis through the charge.

geometry and grid
-----------------
  water depth H = 50.0 m, charge depth 47.0 m, keel line 5.0 m
  grid spacing h = 0.125 m, domain r <= 120.0 m plus 60 sponge cells, simulated time
  0.105 s, water Courant number <= 0.45

charge
------
  W = 300.0 kg TNT equivalent, eta = 1.0
  similitude: K_P = 52.16 MPa, A_P = 1.13, K_T = 92.5 us kg^-1/3, A_T = 0.22
  monopole calibrated at R_ref = 40.0 m: P = 6.9192 MPa, theta = 0.9176 ms, rise 0.15 ms
  bubble: J = 3.38 m kg^-1/3 m^1/3, e_b = 1.6264 MJ/kg

hull element
------------
  areal mass m = 94.2 kg m^-2 (12 mm steel)

provenance
----------
  Similitude constants are the TNT values widely tabulated after Cole
  (1948). Water properties are TEOS-10 through gsw, with salinity read
  as Absolute Salinity and the Baltic composition anomaly ignored.
  Temperature and salinity profiles and seabed constants are round
  values inside the ranges usually quoted for each setting. None is a
  measurement at a site.
