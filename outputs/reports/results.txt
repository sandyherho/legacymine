aged charge
-----------
  Every similitude length scales with (eta W)^(1/3). A charge that
  retains 20 percent of its yield has 0.585 times the hazard radius,
  bubble radius, and bubble period scale of a fresh one, and 5 percent
  retains 0.368 times. Large losses of yield therefore shrink the
  hazard only modestly.
