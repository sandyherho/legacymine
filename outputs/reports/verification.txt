notes
-----
  The exact free-field solution includes the Gaussian source smoothing
  of width 1.5 h. The observed order approaches two, the order of the
  leapfrog step; the fourth-order spatial stencil is not the limiting
  error. The axis flux is written in conservative form, so the
  injected volume is conserved to round-off; a symmetric ghost for r
  u_r lost 1.65 percent of the source at every resolution and was
  rejected. At water Courant numbers near 0.9, closure of cavitated
  water produced growing water-hammer oscillations and a ringing
  precursor ahead of sharp fronts; all runs therefore cap the water
  Courant number at 0.45. The remaining spread of the cavitating keel
  peak, a few percent between Courant numbers 0.3 and 0.6, is the
  uncertainty attached to closure pulses.
